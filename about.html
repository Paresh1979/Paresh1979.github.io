Paresh Founders and Engineers manufactures steel, alloy steel and centrifugal cast components in accordance with customer drawings, material specifications and inspection requirements.

Paresh Founders and Engineers is a trusted manufacturer of steel, alloy steel and centrifugal cast components, supplying castings as per customer drawings, material specifications and inspection requirements.

Established in 2005, we have built our experience around practical foundry knowledge, disciplined manufacturing practices and a strong understanding of industrial casting applications. We serve customers who require reliable steel castings, alloy steel castings, high-temperature components, wear-resistant parts, corrosion-resistant castings and centrifugal casting solutions.

Our strength lies in manufacturing cast components that match customer-specific drawings, grade requirements and application needs. We work closely with customers to understand the end use of each component and provide suitable casting support for fit, function and service reliability.

At Paresh Founders and Engineers, our working approach is based on proper material selection, controlled melting practice, disciplined pouring, practical inspection and clear communication at every stage. This helps us maintain consistency in quality and responsiveness in delivery.

We aim to be a flexible and dependable foundry partner for engineering industries, sponge iron plants, steel plants, cement plants, pump and valve industries, and other industrial sectors requiring engineered steel and alloy steel cast components.
