About Our Steel, Alloy Steel and Centrifugal Casting Foundry

Established in 2005, Paresh Founders and Engineers is an Indian casting manufacturer producing drawing-based components for demanding industrial applications.

Steel casting expertise built since 2005

Paresh Founders and Engineers is a trusted steel casting manufacturer in India, producing alloy steel and centrifugal cast components to customer drawings, material specifications and inspection requirements.

Established in 2005, we have built our experience around practical foundry knowledge, disciplined manufacturing practices and a strong understanding of industrial casting applications. We serve customers who require steel castings, alloy steel castings, heat-resistant and high-alloy castings, wear-resistant parts, corrosion-resistant castings and centrifugal casting solutions.

Our strength lies in manufacturing cast components that match customer-specific drawings, grade requirements and application needs. We work closely with customers to understand the end use of each component and provide suitable casting support for fit, function and service reliability.

Our casting processes are based on proper material selection, controlled melting practice, disciplined pouring, practical inspection and clear communication at every stage. This helps us maintain consistency in quality and responsiveness in delivery.

We aim to be a flexible and dependable foundry partner for cement, sponge iron, steel, furnace, pump, valve and engineering industries requiring engineered steel, alloy steel and centrifugal cast components. Our quality assurance capabilities support customer-specific inspection and documentation requirements.
