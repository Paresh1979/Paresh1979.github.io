Steel Foundry Facilities and Casting Machinery in Murbad

Our Murbad works support raw-material storage, induction melting, sand moulding, core making, centrifugal casting, fettling, finished-casting handling and utility operations. Review our casting processes or send your drawing and capacity requirements.

Steel Casting Plant Infrastructure and Equipment

12,500sq. ft. overall foundry area
6,500sq. ft. casting manufacturing area
750KVA installed power
130KVA available margin

Medium-frequency induction melting units, sand mullers, sand mixer, lip ladles, cooling tower, pneumatic rammers and shop-floor equipment support steel and alloy steel casting production.

Centrifugal casting machines support approximately 66 mm to 1000 mm outside diameter, with a cantilever-type machine for approximately 100 mm to 350 mm outside diameter.

Grinding machines, flexible-shaft grinders, shot blasting, welding support, argon rectifiers, compressors and EOT cranes of 1-ton and 5-ton capacity support fettling and casting handling.

Fume extraction, safety equipment, first aid, fire extinguishers and practical material-handling provisions support foundry operations.
