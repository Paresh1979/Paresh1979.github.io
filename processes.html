Processes

Steel, Alloy Steel and Centrifugal Casting Process

Our casting process is planned around each customer drawing, material grade, dimensions, application, inspection level and delivery requirement.

Drawing & Casting Specification Review

Customer drawings, material grades, dimensions, quantities, service conditions, inspection requirements and delivery expectations are reviewed before manufacturing planning.

Sand & Centrifugal Casting Method Planning

Pattern, core, moulding, risering, gating and process sequence are planned according to component geometry, casting method, material grade and service requirement.

Induction Melting & Chemical Composition Control

Induction melting, controlled charge preparation and spectrometer analysis support carbon steel, low-alloy, stainless steel, heat-resistant, corrosion-resistant and high-alloy castings.

Fettling, Quality Inspection & Dispatch

Runners, risers and fins are removed before castings are finished and checked according to agreed quality and inspection requirements, then dispatched with applicable records and documentation.
